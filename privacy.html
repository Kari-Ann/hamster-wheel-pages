The Hamster Wheel

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Summary

The Hamster Wheel does not collect personal data by default. We collect anonymous usage statistics to understand which parts of the game players reach. These statistics never include your identity, are stored on our own servers in the EU, and are never sold or shared. If you choose to use optional online play, a minimal account is stored on the same server — see "Online Play" below for exactly what that includes.

Data We Collect

Playing offline (solo, hot seat, or the daily challenge) collects no personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) unless you opt into online play
  • No advertising identifiers or location data
  • No cookies or cross-site tracking
  • No IP addresses stored

Local Storage

The game saves your progress (game state, settings, and achievements) locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand which parts of the game players reach, we collect anonymous usage statistics. Each install receives a random, locally-generated identifier that is not linked to your name, account, or email. We send:

  • Progress milestones (game started, week reached, game over with result, trial end reached)
  • Purchases made (which product purchased)
  • Online matches started
  • Invite links opened
  • Daily challenges played or completed

These statistics are stored on our own servers hosted by Fly.io in Stockholm (EU) and are used solely to understand gameplay patterns. They are never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used for cross-app tracking. We do not store IP addresses.

Online Play

Online play — matches against another player over the internet, friend lists, and leaderboards — is entirely optional. The offline game never requires an account.

If you choose to use online play, a minimal account is stored on our own server (the same Fly.io server in Stockholm, EU, mentioned above). That account stores:

  • A display name you choose
  • An email address and password, only if you choose to add them so you can sign back into your account — a guest account created just to play a match stores neither. Your password is never stored as plain text, only as a one-way hash
  • A friend code, generated so other players can add you

This data is used only to run online matches, friends, and leaderboards. It is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment information. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Children's Privacy

The Hamster Wheel does not knowingly collect any information from children. The game is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

Third-Party Services

The game uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and verify entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts from Apple and Google but does not collect advertising identifiers, location data, or personal information. No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are integrated.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page with the new date.
