The Hamster Wheel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

The short version: The Hamster Wheel has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your saved game stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Apple and our purchase provider, RevenueCat, need to process and restore your one-time unlock.

Who We Are

The Hamster Wheel is an indie game developed by Kari Ann Kristiansen. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at kariannolsenkristiansen+hamsterwheel@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

The Hamster Wheel itself collects no personal information and works entirely offline. The only data that gets transmitted anywhere relates to processing your one-time in-app purchase — see Third-Party Services below for details.

Specifically:

  • We do not require an account to play
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising

Local Storage (On Your Device)

The game saves your progress locally on your device using your browser's localStorage (on web) or the device's local storage (on iOS). This data never leaves your device. It includes:

  • Your saved game state (turn, cash, stats, inventory)
  • Your audio and display preferences
  • Your chosen character and cosmetic settings

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The Hamster Wheel is rated 9+ on the App Store. Because the game collects no personal data and the only transmitted data is anonymous purchase verification, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. No data from children under 13 — or anyone else — is collected or transmitted.

Future Online Features (Optional)

We may add optional online features in the future, such as leaderboards or multiplayer. If and when we do:

  • Any online feature will be explicitly optional — the offline game will always be available without creating an account
  • We will update this policy before launching any data-collecting feature
  • We will not collect more data than is strictly necessary
  • We will notify users of the updated policy

Third-Party Services

The game uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. To handle the one-time unlock, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. On every launch, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID; the Apple in-app-purchase receipt and transaction data needed to verify your unlock and restore purchases across devices; and basic device and app metadata (device model, OS version, app version, locale). RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, contacts, location, or advertising identifier, and nothing it receives is used for advertising or sold. Apple itself processes the payment under Apple's own Privacy Policy. The iOS app is distributed through Apple's App Store; Apple's privacy policy applies to that distribution as well.

Data Security

We run no servers or databases of our own that store your data. Purchase verification is handled by Apple and RevenueCat under their own security practices. Your game data lives on your device, protected by your device's own security (iOS file encryption, etc.).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially — for example, when online features are added — we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users through an in-app message.
